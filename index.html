PFCAI Content Automation

This site exists for one purpose: to host the privacy policy, terms of service, and OAuth redirect endpoints required by the LinkedIn and Meta developer platforms for a personal content-automation tool operated by PFCAI. The tool is used by its sole operator to publish his own content to his own social accounts.

There is no product, no sign-up, and no service offered to anyone else here. These pages are static documents and redirect landing pages only.
